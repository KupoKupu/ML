Suppose the dataset $D \subset \set{0, 1}^n$ are binary.  Let $x \in D$ and write \\
 $x = (x_1, x_2,..., x_n)$ for $x_i \in \set{0, 1}$. We can write the likelihood $p(x)$ as
\ali{p(x) = \prod_{i=2}^n p(x_i | x_{< i}) p(x_1),}
where $x_{< i} = (x_1,..., x_{i-1})$.\\
In an autoregressive model we assume the binary $x_i$'s follow Bernoulli distributions. Specifically, $x_1 \sim B(\theta_1)$ and, for $i > 1$,
\ali{p_{\theta_i}(x_i | x_{< i}) = B(f_{\theta_i}(x_{< i})),}
where $\theta_i$'s are trainable parameters and $f_{\theta_i}: \set{0, 1}^{i-1} \ra [0, 1]$.\\
(1) In a {\bf Fully-Visible Sigmoid Belief Network (FVSBN)}, each $f_{\theta_i}$ is just a linear layer:
\ali{f_{\theta_i}(x_{< i}) = \sigma \left(A_i \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ \vdots \\ x_{i-1} \end{pmatrix}\right),}
where $\sigma$ is the sigmoid function and $A_i \in \R^{1 \times (i-1)}$ contains the parameters. Total parameters $\sim O(n^2)$\\
(2) To improve the expressiveness of FVSBN, we may use MLP for each $f_{\theta_i}$:  
\ali{f_{\theta_i}(x_{< i}) &= \sigma(\alpha_i h_i + b_i) \\
        h_i &= \sigma(A_i x_{< i} + c_i) \in \R^d,}
where $\alpha_i \in \R^{1 \times d}$, $b_i \in \R$, $A_i \in \R^{d \times i}$ and $c_i \in \R^d$. Total parameters $\sim O(n^2d)$.\\
(3) {\bf Natural Autoregressive Density Estimator (NADE)} uses a shared weight matrix $W \in \R^{d \times n}$ for all the conditional probabilities:
\ali{f_{\theta_i}(x_{< i}) &= \sigma(\alpha_i h_i + b_i) \\
        h_i &= \sigma(W_{\cdot, <i} \cdot x_{< i} + c).}
Total parameters $\sim O(nd)$.\\
To learn the model our objective function is the KL divergence:
\ali{\min_\theta \KL{p_{data}}{p_\theta} &= \min_\theta \BE_{x \sim p_{data}} \left[ \log p_{data}(x) - \log p_\theta(x) \right] \\
                                        &= \min_\theta \BE_{x \sim p_{data}} \left[ - \log p_\theta(x) \right] \\
                                        &= \max_\theta \BE_{x \sim p_{data}} \left[ \log p_\theta(x) \right],}
which is also to max the likelihood.
